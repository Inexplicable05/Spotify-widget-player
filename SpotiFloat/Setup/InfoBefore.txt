Welcome to SpotiFloat!

SpotiFloat is a beautiful, ultra-sleek floating Spotify widget for Windows that sits elegantly on your desktop (or taskbar) and gives you instant, tactile control over your music. 

Features:
- Micro-Interactions: Beautiful bounce physics on all buttons.
- Dynamic Theming: Hot-swap between Dark, Light, and a frosted Glass Morphism theme!
- Always on Top: Keeps the widget above all other windows (can be toggled off).
- Auto-Hide on Fullscreen: Automatically vanishes when you watch YouTube or play games.

How to Use:
1. Move: Click and drag the widget anywhere on your screen.
2. Settings: Right-click the player or right-click the system tray icon to open the Settings menu.
3. Theme: Open Settings and use the dropdown to switch themes instantly.

Enjoy!
